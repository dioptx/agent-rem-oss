The pile

How this is countedEverything I've ever flagged, at the end of each week: still on my list (Later ones too) vs done (you said fix it, keep it, nah or hush). My list splits in two: seen lately, and gone quiet (no repeat in 30 days; it moves back if it comes up again). Only a few make each morning note, so most of my list never reached you.

Here's what came up

How this worksOne card per thing in my newest morning note. Swipe right, or press Fix it, to take the ticked fix idea (Keep it, for a win). Swipe left for Nah. Swipe up for Later: it comes back in a week. Tap a card, or press Enter on it, to tick a different fix idea or add a note. Nothing is written until you press Save. Saving writes your calls into the morning note and logs them right away. I can't undo a logged call here; edit the morning note file for that. Fixes you take show up next, under Fixes you said yes to.

Fixes you said yes to

How this worksEvery fix idea you said yes to in the last 30 days. Saying yes doesn't make the edit. Copy the ask, paste it into Claude Code, and it makes the edit. Record a change link with: run rem actions --link <thing number> <link>. Things you later said nah to or hushed are left out.

Evidence after acceptance

How this is countedLinked event groups after your first acceptance, excluding the acceptance day. A group contains one session's signals of one kind on one day, not individual errors. Scan coverage means REM scanned a date window; missing transcripts and changes in activity can still affect counts. A linked edit does not confirm installation. These observations do not establish whether a change helped.

Snags, week by week

How this is countedThe share of sessions started each week that hit at least one snag, Claude and Codex apart. A blank week means I was off, I wasn't watching that agent yet, or those sessions are gone (their transcripts were deleted). The overview tiles show each agent separately for the latest full week with measurable session data; each gives the exact week and session denominator. Dotted lines mark a settings change or a fix you took. The second chart counts sessions by the week of their first snag. It needs no session total, so it reaches back to my first night, but it climbs in busy weeks, and older weeks may include noise I've since learned to skip.
